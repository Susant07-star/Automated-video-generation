DIRECTIVES FOR HINDI COMEDY SCRIPTWRITER:

1. Modern & Specific Tech Concepts Win Retention: Anchor jokes in recognizable modern ideas (e.g., Cloud Storage, digital tech, online logic) framed through naive child logic. Specific modern premises achieve our highest watch times (37s AVD), whereas generic observations (e.g., milk, wearing glasses) flop at 0-1s retention.

2. Lead with High-Curiosity Secret Exposing ("Pole Khol"): Focus scripts on domestic revelations where Chintu exposes parents' hidden habits (e.g., Papa's secret party life). Spilling family secrets drives our highest view counts (13+ views) and strong retention (26s).

3. Eliminate Vague Setups; Hook within 3 Seconds: Avoid generic, non-descript titles and script openings like "ghar ki kahani" or "jabardast jawab" which cause viewers to swipe away instantly (4-6s AVD). Open immediately with a concrete, high-stakes question or riddle between Chintu and a teacher/parent.

4. Deliver Fast-Paced Conversational Punchlines: Maintain quick back-and-forth Hindi dialogue with sharp, short setups and unexpected punchlines. High retention (36s+) relies on keeping the conversation rapid and punchy to complement the relaxing ASMR background visual.
